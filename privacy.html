Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026. Last updated 16 November 2026.

1. Who we are

Yanju is a free web app that helps you track your subscriptions, see what they cost, and prepare reports. Yanju is run by Oluwatoyin Olaoluwa in Lagos State, Nigeria. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “Yanju” mean that person. You can reach us at yanjuhq@gmail.com.

This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, who else handles it, and your rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

2. What we collect

  • Account details. Your first name, last name and email address when you create an account. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture details with us. We never see your Google password.
  • Your password. If you sign up with email, your password is handled by our sign-in provider (Supabase). It is stored in a scrambled form and we cannot read it.
  • Subscription details you add. For example the service name, price, currency, billing period, renewal date, category, and any client or project name you type in. These are saved on your own device. If you sign in, they are also saved to your account in our database, so they can follow you to other devices. When you sign out, they are removed from that device but stay in your account. In your account page you can download your data or delete the saved copy.
  • Reminder settings. If you turn on browser notifications, we keep your device’s notification address and the reminder times you choose, so that we can send them.
  • Payments. If you buy a plan, Paystack takes your payment on its own page. We never see or store your card number or bank details. We keep a record of each payment (reference, amount, plan and date) and your plan and its end date on your account.
  • Technical information. Like any website, the services that deliver Yanju can see your IP address and browser type when your device loads the app.

We do not ask for your bank details, card numbers or passwords to other services.

3. Screenshots

If you scan a screenshot to add subscriptions, it is read by your own browser on your own device. It is not uploaded to our servers and we do not keep it.

4. How we use your information

  • To create your account and let you sign in.
  • To show your dashboard, insights, history and reports, and to send reminders you asked for.
  • To suggest alternatives to a service when you ask for them.
  • To keep the app secure and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not show advertising.

5. Other companies that help us run Yanju

We use these services. Each handles information only to provide its part of the app.

  • Supabase provides sign-in and the database where account details are stored.
  • Google provides “Continue with Google” sign-in. We also load small website logos from Google’s icon service, which means Google can see the website address of a service you track.
  • Paystack processes payments for paid plans. It sees your email address and what you paid for.
  • Cloudflare hosts the app and runs our small servers for alternatives and reminders.
  • Exchange Rate API supplies exchange rates. No personal information is sent to it.
  • jsDelivr and Google Fonts deliver code libraries and fonts to your browser.

When you look up alternatives for a service, the name of that service is sent to our server to find suggestions. It is not linked to your account.

Some of these companies are outside Nigeria, so your information may be processed in other countries. We choose providers that use recognised security practices.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account information for as long as you have an account. Data saved only on your device stays there until you clear it. If you ask us to delete your account, we will delete your account details and saved subscriptions, except where the law requires us to keep something.

7. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you may ask us to give you a copy of your information, correct it, delete it, or stop using it for a purpose. You may also withdraw your consent at any time. To do any of these, email yanjuhq@gmail.com. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

8. Security

We use sign-in and database protections so that each person can see only their own saved data. No system is completely secure, so please choose a strong password and keep it private.

9. Children

Yanju is not meant for anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, tell us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Yanju grows. When we make an important change, we will update the date at the top of this page.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: yanjuhq@gmail.com.
